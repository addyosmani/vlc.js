About VLC.js

VLC.js media player
WebAssembly Edition

VLC.js is a port of VLC media player to WebAssembly, built with Emscripten.
Supports MKV, AVI, DVD menus, 10-bit video, SSA subtitles, and more.

Based on VLC media player by VideoLAN
